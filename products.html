OUR PRODUCTS

Flour Products

Tara Grains provides a range of flour products designed for different food preparation and processing requirements.

Maida

Refined wheat flour suitable for a variety of bakery, food preparation and cooking applications.

  • Fine refined flour
  • Suitable for bakery products
  • Useful for cooking applications
  • Consistent texture

Wheat Atta

Wheat flour suitable for preparing everyday food products such as roti, chapati and other wheat-based dishes.

  • Made from wheat
  • Suitable for chapati and roti
  • Everyday cooking use
  • Consistent flour quality

Wheat Bran

The outer layer separated during wheat processing and milling, commonly used in food and feed applications.

  • Obtained during milling
  • Useful by-product
  • Food applications
  • Feed applications
